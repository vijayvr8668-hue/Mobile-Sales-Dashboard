# Mobile-Sales-Dashboard
Developed an interactive Mobile Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze mobile sales performance and gain meaningful business insights. The dashboard provides a clear view of total sales, quantity sold, transaction count, and average selling price.

## Key Insights

1. **Sales Performance Analysis:** Analyzed total mobile sales, total quantity sold, total transactions, and average selling price to understand overall business performance.

2. **Brand-wise Sales Analysis:** Compared sales performance across different mobile brands to identify top-performing brands.

3. **City-wise Sales Analysis:** Evaluated mobile sales across different cities to understand geographical sales distribution.

4. **Monthly Sales Trends:** Analyzed month-wise sales trends to identify changes in sales performance over time.

5. **Payment Method Analysis:** Studied different payment methods to understand customer payment preferences.

6. **Customer Rating Analysis:** Evaluated customer ratings to understand customer satisfaction and purchasing experience.

7. **Interactive Dashboard:** Used charts, slicers, and filters to enable users to explore sales data and generate meaningful insights.

## Why This Project Stands Out
- Interactive and user-friendly dashboard.
- Provides meaningful sales insights.
- Analyzes brand, city, and customer trends.
- Uses Power Query and DAX for data analysis.
- Supports data-driven business decisions.
